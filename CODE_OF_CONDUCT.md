# Code of Conduct

LRSolid aims to be a professional, inclusive, and useful research software project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about problems.
- Keep technical discussion focused on evidence, reproducibility, and maintainability.
- Give appropriate credit for ideas, code, documentation, datasets, and research outputs.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and institutional constraints.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or hostile language.
- Publishing private communication, credentials, personal data, or security details without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical or research discussion.
- Misrepresenting authorship, affiliation, licensing, funding, or research provenance.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer privately where possible. If private contact is not available, use the least public channel available and avoid sharing sensitive personal or security information in public issues.

Reports will be handled with care, confidentiality where practical, and attention to the safety of affected people.
